My best Movies

My top 3 movies of all the time.

The Vikings

It's my favorite because it makes me feel the freedom from the modern routine. How our ancestors lived once befors us. And how strong is the blood bond between the family members no matter their disagreements.

The Pirates of the Caribbeans

Pirates of the Caribbean is just pure, effortless fun that completely pulls you away from everyday life and drops you right into the middle of the open ocean. Between the cursed gold, the wild sword fights, and Jack Sparrow's chaotic charm, it turns the high seas into the ultimate adventure. It has this incredible way of making you feel completely free, reminding you of a time when the horizon was wide open and anything could happen.

The 100

what i love most about the show is how a disease devastates the planet, forcing humanity to escape and live on a space station that simulates Earth. When a group of teenagers commit serious crimes, their punishment is normally execution, but after a deep discussion among the station's rulers, they decide to send the teens to Earth as an experiment to see if the planet is still survivable.
